In progress

TVC rocket

We created our Thrust vectoring rocket in Onshape and programmed it in C++. It uses a Teensy 4.1 for computing flight data and a Sparkfun BMI260 IMU for acquring position and rotation data. The fin can, nose cone, and gimbal are 3d printed in PA6-GF, and the main tube is a 3 foot long piece of 75 mm blue tube. The Onshape document, BOM, OpenRocket files, and code can all be found here.
